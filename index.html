Research

The three-dimensional distribution of galaxies encodes the physics of the Universe from its earliest moments to today. We can study the large-scale structure of the universe using summary statistics called the N-point correlation functions. The simplest statistic is the two-point correlation function, however adding higher-order statistics to the analysis allows us to extract more information from the data. Starting from the three-point correlation function, we can access the non-gaussian information encoded in the galaxy distribution.
